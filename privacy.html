ZCare Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

ZCare respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ZCare application.

Information You Provide

ZCare may process information that you choose to provide while using the app, such as account information, appointments, notes, and other information required to provide the app's features.

Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use, ZCare may request permission to access certain features of your device, including contacts, calendar, microphone, photos, and location.

These permissions are requested only when necessary for the relevant functionality. You can manage device permissions through your device settings.

Appointments and Calendar

ZCare can help users create and manage appointments and reminders. When you choose to add an appointment to your device calendar, ZCare may access the calendar as required to perform that action.

Contacts

If you choose to use features involving contacts, ZCare may request access to your device contacts so that you can select or interact with contacts through supported features.

Microphone and Voice Features

Some ZCare features may use the microphone for voice input. Microphone access is only used when required for voice-enabled features and when permission has been granted by the user.

Location

Certain features may request access to location information when location is required to provide the requested functionality. Location access is subject to the permissions you grant on your device.

Photos

ZCare may request access to photos when you choose to use a feature that requires selecting or accessing an image. Access is subject to your device permissions.

Account Information

If you create a ZCare account, information such as your name, email address, and authentication information may be processed as necessary to create, secure, and manage your account.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information processed through ZCare. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

ZCare may rely on third-party services necessary to operate certain features of the application. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Children's Privacy

ZCare is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is required, appropriate steps will be taken in accordance with applicable law.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Your Choices

You can control permissions granted to ZCare through your device settings. Where applicable, you may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ZCare's privacy practices, please contact the ZCare support team through the official ZCare support page.
